# 01: Test Rocket Copilot Contract Review directly

**What to build:** A first-hand, recorded answer to what Rocket Copilot Contract Review actually does,
to replace an inference drawn from a press release (PRD §4 test 6, ADR-0002 Amendment 2). The product
is free and the test costs an afternoon. The reopened buyer decision cannot close without it: if Rocket
Copilot quotes exact sentences and drafts counter-offers, Redline's paid claim does not exist.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately).

**Status:** ready-for-human

- [ ] At least one real freelance service agreement is run through Rocket Copilot Contract Review.
      Ideally it contains a known dangerous clause and says nothing about payment timing.
- [ ] Use a Document you are free to share with a third party. Nothing confidential.
- [ ] Recorded, with screenshots: does each finding quote the exact sentence it came from, verbatim?
- [ ] Recorded, with screenshots: does it draft replacement language the user could send?
- [ ] Recorded: can the user state their own red lines and have them drive the analysis? This is the
      third differentiator claimed in ADR-0002 Amendment 1.
- [ ] Also noted: whether it raises the missing payment timing, and what the free tier gives versus
      what sits behind the Rocket Legal+ membership.
- [ ] The result is written up as a dated amendment to ADR-0002. It answers item 1 of that ADR's
      "What has to be settled before this closes".
