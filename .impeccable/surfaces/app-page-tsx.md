# Landing page

## Scope

The public landing page at the site root. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

## Brief

- **Visitor:** the Signer in PRODUCT.md, holding a client's agreement they have not signed yet
  and can still push back on.
- **What they should believe:** every flag Redline raises points at a sentence they can find in
  their own copy.
- **One demonstration:** a sample agreement turning into ranked flags, each showing the exact
  sentence it came from.
- **One action:** "Try it on a document", linking to the signed-in app entry.
- **Proof on hand:** the demonstration itself, on a sample agreement written for this page and
  labeled as a sample. No prices, customers, testimonials or quotes.
- **Never claims:** a verdict on whether to sign, legal advice, support for scanned or
  photographed documents, or document types outside freelance and client service agreements.
  Copy may say that each flag comes with drafted wording to send back.
- **Copy:** every visible line goes through the humanizer skill before it is committed.
- **Ruled out by the user:** legal-tech SaaS, scare tactics, chatbot, law-firm gravitas.

## Memorable moment

Pulling a flag: it slides out from the page edge and its tape lays across the exact line it came
from.

## Unresolved

- The app entry route behind "Try it on a document" is not built yet.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The agreement is a printed page on a desk, and Redline's findings are flag tabs stuck on
the exact lines they come from, numbered by rank. It refuses the category default of a headline
beside a floating dashboard screenshot.

OWN-WORLD: A slate felt desk blotter owns the ground. On it, a bright white Letter page set in a
Times-style contract face. Translucent magenta flag tape whose tip lays over the cited line. Black
ink, Archivo for Redline's own voice, one row rhythm. Cyan is reserved for the reader's own marks:
focus and selection.

STORY: The visitor sees a real-looking agreement with three flags on specific lines. They pull one
and read a plain-English line beside the same sentence lit on the page. They come away believing
every flag can be checked, and click "Try it on a document".

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left third: wordmark, headline, one line of support, and the "Try it on a document"
button, all above the fold. Center and right: the page as large as the first viewport allows beside
the ranked list (480px wide at 1440, about 60% of Letter; amended at finish review, since life size
cannot share a 900px viewport with the list). Three flags sit on its right edge at their lines,
numbered 1 to 3. Flag 1 is pulled open by default, with its note in the ranked list beside the page.
At phone width the page is cropped to the flagged sections under a cut edge, so a tab reaches the
first screen and the ranked list sits directly under it.

FORM: Page Flags, position 1 of 7 on the ordered list (the pick card), chosen over the rolled
Facts Panel; seed key 473d9d8a.

SIGNATURE: Pulling a flag slides it further out and wipes its tape across the cited line, left to
right, ease-out, about 300ms. Reduced motion shows the end state.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
